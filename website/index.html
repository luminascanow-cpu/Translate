Perfect For Every Journey

The Traveler

Visiting Tamil Nadu or Maharashtra? Open the BhashaSethu OCR camera, point it at a street sign or menu, and instantly see the English translation overlaid perfectly.

The Language Learner

Overwhelmed by complex alphabets? Use the dedicated Learn Tab to explore a complete, categorized breakdown of characters. Tap any letter to hear its exact pronunciation.
