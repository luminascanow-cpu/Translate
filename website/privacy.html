Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 6, 2026

Welcome to BhashaSethu! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information processed through our mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information when you use our mobile app ("BhashaSethu") and services.

1. Information We Process

We practice data minimization. The types of data processed include:

  • Images and Camera Data: The app requires access to your camera to capture text for translation (OCR). We do not upload or store your images on our servers. Captured images are processed securely in real-time by the AI translation APIs and immediately discarded.
  • Local Translation History: Your translation log, bookmarks, and favorite phrases are stored locally on your device. We do not sync, store, or back up this data to the cloud.
  • Subscription and Purchase Data (RevenueCat): If you purchase BhashaSethu Pro, transaction metadata and subscription entitlement status are processed securely through our partner, RevenueCat. RevenueCat does not store your credit card details.
  • Device & Ad Identifiers (Google AdMob): Free tier users may see advertisements. We use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, and telemetry data to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide high-quality translation, text-to-speech, billing, and ad-serving capabilities, we work with the following trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Gemini API: For optical character recognition (OCR) and text translation.
  • Sarvam AI: For natural text-to-speech voice generation.
  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app purchases, subscriptions, and checking Pro entitlements. (Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy)
  • Google AdMob: For serving reward ads and promotional content on the free tier. (Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy)

3. Data Retention and Security

Because we do not require user accounts, we do not collect or store personal profiles, names, email addresses, or passwords on our servers. Transmission of translation requests from your device to the translation APIs is secured using industry-standard SSL/HTTPS encryption.

4. Children's Privacy

BhashaSethu does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
