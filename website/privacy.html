Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 29, 2026

Welcome to BhashaSethu! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. The information we process includes:

  • Images and Camera Data: Our app requires access to your camera to perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR). We do NOT store your photos on our servers. Images are processed instantly for translation purposes and discarded unless you explicitly choose to save a translation locally on your device.
  • Translation History: The translations you choose to save (History & Favorites) are stored locally on your device. We do not sync this data to the cloud.
  • App Preferences: We store your language preferences (e.g., source and target languages) locally to improve your app experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data we process is used exclusively to:

  • Provide real-time OCR translation and transliteration services.
  • Generate native text-to-speech audio pronunciation.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the BhashaSethu app.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide accurate translations and pronunciation, we utilize secure, industry-leading AI APIs (such as Gemini and Sarvam AI). When a text is translated, the text payload is sent securely to these APIs. No personally identifiable information is attached to these requests.

4. Data Security

We use robust security measures to protect the data transmitted from your device to our translation servers. Since we do not require user accounts, we do not store names, email addresses, or passwords on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for educational use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
